Each note is one problem that took a while to understand, and what the
repository does about it now. They are here because the answer lives in
several files at once — a config, a script, a CI job — and none of those
files is the right place to explain it.

They are not the other half of the [tool pages](/docs/). Those are readmes
kept beside the configuration they document, and they run to the editor and
the per-language setups. The `PATH`, the window manager and mise, which
most of these notes are about, have no page there. One note ends with
nothing configured at all, because the diagnosis was wrong.

The timings and counts come from one machine, described in
[The machine](/machine/), so you can tell which of them would hold on
yours.
